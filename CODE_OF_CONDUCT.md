# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in ReliefOS harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behaviour include empathy, respectful disagreement, constructive feedback,
accountability, and prioritizing the dignity and privacy of disaster-affected people.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, threats, publishing another person's private
information, exploiting sensitive disaster material, discriminatory language, or conduct that
could endanger affected people or responders.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and communications that violate
this code and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors. Report conduct concerns privately
to the repository maintainers. Maintainers will respect the reporter's privacy and respond in a
fair and timely manner.

This code is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
